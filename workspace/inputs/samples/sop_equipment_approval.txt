STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
SOP-EA-2019-004
Equipment Repair Approval Process

1. SCOPE
This SOP covers the approval process for major equipment repairs exceeding Rs. 5 lakh in cost or requiring more than 48 hours of downtime.

2. RESPONSIBILITIES
2.1 Equipment Inspector: Initiates the repair request with detailed findings and recommendations.
2.2 Maintenance Engineer: Reviews technical feasibility and cost estimate.
2.3 Plant Manager: Final approval for repairs under Rs. 50 lakh.
2.4 Safety Officer: Mandatory co-sign for repairs involving pressure vessels or safety systems.

3. PROCEDURE
3.1 Inspector submits Equipment Inspection Report (Form IR-xx) with findings and recommendations.
3.2 Maintenance Engineer prepares cost estimate and downtime analysis within 5 working days.
3.3 Joint review meeting: Inspector, Maintenance Engineer, and Safety Officer (if applicable).
3.4 Draft approval note prepared using template APPR-2024, including summary of findings, cost estimate, downtime impact, safety implications, and recommended action.
3.5 Plant Manager reviews and signs. Safety Officer co-signs if pressure vessel involved.
3.6 Approved note forwarded to Procurement for execution.

4. DOCUMENTATION
All approvals must reference the original inspection report number and be filed in the Equipment History file.

5. REVISION HISTORY
Rev 0: 2019-03-15, Initial issue
Rev 1: 2021-06-20, Added Safety Officer co-sign requirement
